# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

UK families who employ a nanny, au pair or household staff (they set the arrangement up and pay). The staff themselves, who check shifts, agreements and house information on their phone, often in a hurry. Agencies, who hand out seats to the families they place.

## Product Purpose

One private place for the whole household arrangement: shared calendar, agreements, timesheets, a documents vault, house codes and emergency information, replacing spreadsheets and group chats.

## Positioning

Access by private link and code (no password), agencies can distribute seats to their families, and a family can export everything and take their data with them when they leave.

## Operating Context

One website that also runs inside an iOS and Android app shell (Capacitor, loading familywallet.io). UK English. Phones are the main device for staff; parents use phone and desktop.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Link-plus-code and magic-link sign-in, no SMS. Agency seat distribution and independent family plan. Lifecycle for locked wallets: 30-day export window, reminders, then deletion. i18n catalogue with en-GB as the base. Legal wording belongs to the owner or their solicitor.

## Brand Commitments

Approved 12 Nov 2026 (v2): a light ice-white ground with soft sky-blue and mint washes, translucent glass cards, navy for figures. Keep the serif headings (Playfair Display with Plus Jakarta Sans), the green Family Wallet logo and green for the one main action per screen, red only for Emergency, and a calm, trustworthy tone. Motion is quiet and purposeful, and respects reduced-motion settings.

## Evidence on Hand

No testimonials, customer logos or benchmarks exist; none may be invented.

## Product Principles

Calm over clever: a household is a sensitive place. The next thing to do is always obvious. Privacy is visible, never buried. Built for one thumb on a phone first.
